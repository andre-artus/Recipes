# Combined hands-on prep: three chicken dishes

**For 2 portions each:** Thai Yellow Curry Chicken, Smoky Chicken Fajita Wraps and Joojeh Kebab.

Work through this list by ingredient, doing all the washing, peeling, trimming, chopping, grating and juicing together. Put the prepared portions into labelled containers for each dish. Measure oils, yoghurt, sauces and dry seasonings when making the individual recipes.

## Main prep list

| Ingredient | Total to prepare | Prep and allocation |
|---|---|---|
| **Onions** | **1 small + 1½ medium**; about **305 g peeled** | Peel all together. **Curry:** 1 small (80 g), cut into wedges. **Fajitas:** 1 medium (150 g), sliced. **Joojeh:** ½ medium (about 75 g), thinly sliced. |
| **Carrots — added variation** | Suggested **200 g trimmed**, roughly **2 medium carrots** | Wash, trim and peel all together. **Curry:** 100 g, cut into **5 mm rounds or half-moons**. **Fajitas:** 100 g, cut into **thin diagonal slices, about 2 mm thick**; halve lengthways first if large. Keep the two portions separate. |
| **Potatoes** | **200 g** | Wash, peel and cut into **1.5 cm cubes**, all for **curry**. If preparing ahead, cover with cold water and refrigerate; drain before cooking. |
| **Sweet/bell pepper** | **1 large**, about **180 g** | Wash, remove stalk, seeds and white core, then slice into strips; all for **fajitas**. |
| **Garlic** | **1 clove** | Peel and finely grate; all for **fajita chicken**. |
| **Fresh coriander** | **10 g** | Wash, dry and chop leaves and tender stems; all for **fajita lime yoghurt**. Keep separate from raw chicken. |
| **Limes** | Enough for **37.5 ml juice + zest of ½ lime**; have **2 limes** available and check juice yield | Wash and dry. Finely grate the zest of **½ lime for Joojeh**, then juice. Reserve **25 ml juice for fajitas** (10 ml for yoghurt, 15 ml to finish the cooked filling) and **12.5 ml for Joojeh**. Keep zest separate. |
| **Boneless, skinless chicken** | **950 g total** | Trim as needed. **Curry:** 300 g thighs, cut into **3 cm pieces**. **Fajitas:** 350 g breast, cut into **thin strips**. **Joojeh:** 300 g thighs or breast, cut into **3 cm pieces**. Keep the three portions separate. |

The Joojeh onion weight is approximate; its recipe specifies half a medium onion. For chicken, use **600 g thighs + 350 g breast** if choosing thighs for Joojeh, or **300 g thighs + 650 g breast** if choosing breast for it.

Carrots are your addition to the curry and fajitas, rather than an ingredient in the source recipes. The suggested 100 g per dish is for two portions and can be adjusted. Add the curry carrots with the potatoes; cook the thin fajita carrot slices with the onion and pepper, allowing a little extra time if needed.

## Other hands-on prep

- **Joojeh saffron:** grind 1 small pinch of saffron threads with the recipe's ⅛ tsp salt. Bloom with 1 Tbsp (15 ml) boiling water when preparing the marinade. The salt is part of the recipe's total, not extra.

## Optional prep

- **Curry fried-shallot garnish:** if making it, peel enough shallots to give **50 g peeled**, slice evenly about **1 mm thick**, separate the slices and pat thoroughly dry. Do this alongside the onion prep, but keep them separate. Fry according to the [fried-shallot recipe](Thai/Crispy%20Fried%20Shallots.md).
- **Fajita avocado:** **1 small**, optional. Leave whole until serving, then halve, remove the stone, peel and slice.
- **Fajita lime wedges:** reserve extra lime if wanted; these are additional to the measured juice above.
- **Curry tamarind:** if using ready-made paste, there is no chopping prep. If making the optional paste from a block, break up **10 g pulp**, soak in **20 ml freshly boiled water for 20 minutes**, then mash and sieve, rinsing with **5 ml water**. Finish by simmering as directed in the [tamarind paste recipe](Thai/Tamarind%20Paste.md).

## Suggested order

1. Label containers **Curry**, **Fajitas** and **Joojeh**. Have separate containers for raw chicken and ready-to-eat coriander/lime.
2. Wash the carrots, potatoes, pepper, coriander and limes; dry the coriander and limes.
3. Peel and cut **all onions**, plus optional shallots. Divide by dish and cut style.
4. Trim, peel and slice **all carrots**, dividing between curry and fajitas as above. Prepare the **pepper, garlic and coriander**.
5. **Zest, then juice the limes**; divide by dish. Keep the fajita sauce juice and finishing juice separate if convenient.
6. Peel and cube the **potatoes**; cover with cold water and refrigerate if not cooking immediately.
7. Trim, cut and portion **all chicken last**. Cover and refrigerate the portions; clean the knife, board and work surface afterwards.

Keep the prepared ingredients separate until assembling each recipe. The curry paste is already made, so none of its ingredients need further prep. The fajita recipe uses dried chilli powder/flakes, so **there are no fresh chillies to chop** in these three recipes.
