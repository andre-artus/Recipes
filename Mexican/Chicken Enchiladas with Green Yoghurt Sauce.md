# Chicken Enchiladas with Green Yoghurt Sauce

Mexican-inspired baked chicken wraps with tomato sauce, melted cheese and a fresh coriander-lime yoghurt topping.

**Serves:** 2 (2 enchiladas each)  
**Prep:** 20 minutes  
**Cook:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

### Poached chicken

- 350 g skinless, boneless chicken breast
- Water, enough to cover the chicken

### Tomato sauce

- 2 tsp (10 ml) cooking oil
- 1 small onion (about 100 g), finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked or ordinary paprika
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
- 400 g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 3 Tbsp + 1 tsp (50 ml) water
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) fine salt, or to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

### Green yoghurt sauce

- 120 g plain yoghurt
- 15 g fresh coriander (cilantro), including tender stems
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice, plus more to taste
- Finely grated zest of 1 lime
- 1 small garlic clove
- Half a green chilli, deseeded if preferred (optional)
- A pinch of salt

### To assemble

- 4 medium flour wraps (about 20 cm across)
- 80 g cheddar or gouda, grated

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan. Use a baking dish roughly 20 × 25 cm, or one that holds the four rolled wraps snugly.
2. Put the chicken in a saucepan and cover with water. Bring just to a gentle simmer, then cook gently for about 12–18 minutes, depending on thickness, until the thickest part reaches 75°C. Remove, rest for 5 minutes, then shred with two forks. Prepare the tomato sauce while the chicken cooks.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Soften the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and oregano and stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add the tomatoes, 3 Tbsp + 1 tsp (50 ml) water, salt and pepper. Simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
5. Blend the green yoghurt sauce ingredients until smooth, or finely chop the coriander, garlic and chilli and stir everything together. Taste and adjust the lime and salt. Keep refrigerated.
6. Mix the shredded chicken with about one third of the tomato sauce and 30 g of the green yoghurt sauce. Spread another third of the tomato sauce over the base of the baking dish.
7. Warm the wraps briefly so they bend easily. Divide the chicken filling between them, roll up and place seam-side down in the dish. Spoon over the remaining tomato sauce and scatter with cheese.
8. Bake for 15–20 minutes, until hot throughout and the cheese is melted and lightly golden. Serve with the remaining green yoghurt sauce spooned over at the table.

## Notes

- Most of the yoghurt sauce goes on after baking to keep it smooth and fresh.
- This uses an easy tomato sauce rather than a traditional tomatillo sauce; no specialist ingredients are needed.
