# Chicken Tortilla Soup

A substantial tomato-based chicken soup with beans, frozen sweetcorn and crisp wrap strips.

**Serves:** 2 generous main-course portions  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

### Crisp wrap strips

- 1 medium flour wrap (about 20 cm across)
- 1 tsp (5 ml) cooking oil
- A small pinch of salt

### Soup

- 2 tsp (10 ml) cooking oil
- 1 small onion (about 100 g), chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked or ordinary paprika
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) chilli powder or flakes, or to taste
- 400 g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 500 ml chicken stock
- 300 g skinless, boneless chicken breast
- 120 g tinned kidney beans or black beans, drained and rinsed (drained weight)
- 100 g frozen sweetcorn
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice, plus more to taste
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve

- 80 g plain yoghurt
- 10 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- Lime wedges (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan. Cut the wrap into strips about 1 cm wide, toss with 1 tsp (5 ml) oil and a small pinch of salt, and spread on a baking tray. Bake for 6–10 minutes, turning once and checking early, until golden and crisp. Set aside.
2. Meanwhile, heat 2 tsp (10 ml) oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Fry the onion for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, oregano and chilli and stir for 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes and stock and bring to a gentle simmer. Add the chicken breasts, making sure they are mostly submerged. Cover loosely and simmer gently for 12–18 minutes, turning once, until the thickest part reaches 75°C. Avoid a hard boil, which can toughen the chicken.
4. Transfer the chicken to a board. Add the beans and frozen sweetcorn to the soup and simmer for 5–7 minutes, or until the corn is cooked according to its packet instructions.
5. Shred the chicken with two forks and return it to the soup. Heat through for 1–2 minutes. Add 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice, then taste before adding salt and pepper: stock can already be quite salty.
6. Divide between two large bowls. Top with yoghurt, coriander and the crisp wrap strips just before eating. Serve with optional lime wedges.

## Notes

- For a thicker soup, mash some of the beans against the side of the saucepan before returning the chicken.
- If the soup becomes thicker than you like, add a little water or stock.
- To make the strips without an oven, toast the whole wrap in a dry frying pan over medium heat until crisp on both sides, then break into pieces. Omit the 1 tsp (5 ml) oil for the strips.
