# Chicken and Sweetcorn Quesadillas

**Serves:** 2 (1 large folded quesadilla each)  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

### Quick salsa

- 1 medium tomato (about 150 g), finely chopped
- 30 g red onion, finely chopped
- 5 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice
- A pinch of salt

### Lime yoghurt

- 80 g plain yoghurt
- 1 tsp (5 ml) lime juice

### Quesadilla filling

- 300 g skinless, boneless chicken breast, cut into small pieces
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked or ordinary paprika
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) chilli powder, or to taste
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) fine salt, or to taste
- 2 tsp (10 ml) cooking oil
- 100 g frozen sweetcorn
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) water
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced

### To assemble

- 100 g cheddar or gouda, grated
- 2 large flour wraps (about 25–30 cm across)

## Method

1. Mix the tomato, red onion, coriander, 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice and a pinch of salt. Set aside. Mix the yoghurt with the remaining 1 tsp (5 ml) lime juice in a separate bowl.
2. Toss the chicken with the cumin, paprika, chilli and salt. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the chicken for 5–7 minutes, stirring, until cooked through; the thickest piece should reach 75°C. Transfer to a bowl.
3. Put the frozen sweetcorn into the same pan with 1 Tbsp (15 ml) water. Cook for 3–5 minutes, or according to the packet instructions, until hot and tender. Let any remaining water evaporate. Stir in the spring onions for 30 seconds, then add to the chicken.
4. Wipe out the pan if needed and reduce the heat to medium-low. Sprinkle a quarter of the cheese over half of each wrap. Divide the chicken mixture over the cheese, then add the remaining cheese. Fold the empty half of each wrap over the filling.
5. Toast one folded wrap at a time in the dry pan for 2–3 minutes per side, pressing lightly with a spatula, until golden and the cheese has melted. Lower the heat if the wrap browns too quickly.
6. Cut into wedges and serve with the salsa and lime yoghurt.

## Notes

- If your wraps are only about 20 cm across, use 4 wraps and make 4 smaller folded quesadillas.
- You can use 225 g cooked, chopped chicken instead of the raw chicken: warm it with the spices and a splash of water before adding the cooked sweetcorn.
