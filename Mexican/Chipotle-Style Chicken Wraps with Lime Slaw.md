# Chipotle-Style Chicken Wraps with Lime Slaw

Smoked paprika and chilli give these wraps a chipotle-style flavour using everyday spices.

**Serves:** 2 (2 wraps each)  
**Prep:** 20 minutes  
**Cook:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

### Lime slaw

- 150 g cabbage, finely shredded
- 50 g carrot, coarsely grated
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice
- 1 tsp (5 ml) cooking or olive oil
- 5 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- A pinch of salt

### Lime yoghurt

- 100 g plain yoghurt
- 1 tsp (5 ml) lime juice
- 5 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped

### Smoky chicken

- 2 tsp (10 ml) cooking oil
- 1 small onion (about 100 g), thinly sliced
- 350 g skinless, boneless chicken breast, cut into thin strips
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 tsp (10 ml) smoked paprika
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
- ¼–½ tsp (1.25–2.5 ml) chilli powder or flakes, to taste
- 20 g tomato paste
- 4 Tbsp (60 ml) water
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) fine salt, or to taste
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice

### To serve

- 4 medium flour wraps (about 20 cm across)

## Method

1. Toss the cabbage and carrot with 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice, 1 tsp (5 ml) oil, 5 g coriander and a pinch of salt. Gently squeeze with clean hands for about 30 seconds, then leave to soften while you cook.
2. Mix the yoghurt with 1 tsp (5 ml) lime juice and the remaining 5 g coriander. Set aside in the fridge.
3. Heat 2 tsp (10 ml) oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and fry for 4–5 minutes, until softened.
4. Add the chicken and fry for 4–5 minutes, turning occasionally, until lightly browned. Add the garlic, paprika, cumin, oregano and chilli; stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add the tomato paste, water and salt. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce coats the chicken and the thickest piece of chicken reaches 75°C. Add a little more water if the pan becomes dry before the chicken is cooked.
6. Remove from the heat and stir in 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice. Taste and adjust the salt or chilli.
7. Warm the wraps in a dry frying pan. Divide the slaw and chicken between them, spoon over the yoghurt sauce and fold to serve.

## Notes

- If you have chipotle paste, use 1–2 tsp (5–10 ml) in place of the chilli powder and reduce the smoked paprika to 1 tsp (5 ml). Adjust to taste: brands vary in heat.
- Drain off excess liquid from the slaw before filling the wraps.
