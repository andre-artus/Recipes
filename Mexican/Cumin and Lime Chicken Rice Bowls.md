# Cumin and Lime Chicken Rice Bowls

**Serves:** 2  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

### Rice

- 120 g uncooked long-grain or basmati rice
- Water, according to the rice packet instructions
- A pinch of salt

### Lime yoghurt

- 100 g plain yoghurt
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice
- A pinch of salt

### Tomato salsa

- 1 medium tomato (about 150 g), chopped
- 30 g red onion, finely chopped
- 5 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice

### Chicken

- 300 g skinless, boneless chicken breast, cut into strips
- 2 tsp (10 ml) cooking oil
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked or ordinary paprika
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) fine salt, or to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice

### Sweetcorn and beans

- 100 g frozen sweetcorn
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) water
- 120 g tinned kidney beans or black beans, drained and rinsed (drained weight)

### To serve

- 1 small avocado, sliced (optional)
- 5 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- Hot sauce, to taste (optional)

## Method

1. Cook the rice according to the packet instructions, adding a pinch of salt. Once cooked, leave covered until ready to serve.
2. Mix the yoghurt, 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice and a pinch of salt. In another bowl, combine all the tomato salsa ingredients.
3. Toss the chicken with the oil, cumin, paprika, oregano, garlic, ½ tsp (2.5 ml) salt and pepper.
4. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the chicken for 5–7 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and cooked through; the thickest piece should reach 75°C. Remove from the heat, add 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice and transfer to a plate.
5. Add the frozen sweetcorn and 2 Tbsp (30 ml) water to the pan. Cook for 3–5 minutes, or according to the packet instructions, until tender. Stir in the drained beans and heat for another 2–3 minutes, adding a splash of water if necessary.
6. Divide the rice between two bowls. Add the chicken, beans, sweetcorn, tomato salsa and optional avocado. Spoon over the lime yoghurt and finish with the coriander listed under "To serve" and optional hot sauce.

## Notes

- Kidney beans are an easy substitute for black beans; use tinned, ready-cooked beans for this recipe.
- Freeze any leftover drained beans from the tin for another bowl or a batch of tortilla soup.
