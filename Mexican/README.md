# Mexican-Inspired Chicken Recipes

All recipes serve **2 adults** and use chicken breast and ingredients commonly available in South African supermarkets.

## Ingredient and measurement notes

- Ingredients and ingredient sections are listed in the order they are first used in the method.
- Small volumes use **tsp** for teaspoon and **Tbsp** for tablespoon, with the metric volume in brackets: **1 tsp (5 ml)**, **½ tsp (2.5 ml)** and **1 Tbsp (15 ml)**. Larger liquid volumes remain in ml.
- Coriander and cilantro are the same herb; these recipes use the fresh leaves and tender stems. Dried oregano is used where specified. Basil is not needed.
- Use plain, unsweetened yoghurt. Full-fat yoghurt gives a richer sauce.
- Sweetcorn quantities refer to **frozen kernels, weighed from frozen**. There is no need to thaw them; cook according to the recipe and packet instructions.
- Bean quantities are **drained weights**. Tinned kidney beans and black beans are interchangeable here.
- Cheddar or gouda works for the quesadillas and enchiladas.
- Lime sizes vary: the recipes specify measured juice quantities, with ml alongside the spoon measures.
- Chilli powder means ground chilli or a blend whose ingredients you know. Start with less if yours is particularly hot.
- Chicken cooking times are guides; check that the thickest part reaches **75°C**.
