# Smoky Chicken Fajita Wraps

**Serves:** 2 (2 wraps each)  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

### Lime yoghurt

- 100 g plain yoghurt
- 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice, plus more to taste
- 10 g fresh coriander (cilantro), chopped
- A pinch of salt

### Chicken and vegetables

- 350 g skinless, boneless chicken breast, sliced into thin strips
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) cooking oil, divided
- 2 tsp (10 ml) smoked paprika
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) chilli powder or flakes, or to taste
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) fine salt, or to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1 medium onion (about 150 g), sliced
- 1 large pepper (about 180 g), sliced
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice

### To serve

- 4 medium flour wraps (about 20 cm across)
- 1 small avocado, sliced (optional)
- Lime wedges (optional)

## Method

1. Mix the yoghurt, 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice, coriander and a pinch of salt. Refrigerate until needed.
2. Toss the chicken with 2 tsp (10 ml) oil, the paprika, cumin, oregano, chilli, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. Heat the remaining 1 tsp (5 ml) oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the onion and pepper for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and browned at the edges. Transfer to a plate.
4. Add the chicken to the hot pan in a single layer, working in batches if necessary. Fry for 5–7 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and cooked through. The centre of the thickest strip should reach 75°C.
5. Return the vegetables to the pan and toss with the chicken for 1 minute. Remove from the heat and add 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lime juice.
6. Warm the wraps in a dry frying pan for about 20 seconds per side. Divide the chicken and vegetables between them and add the yoghurt sauce and optional avocado. Fold and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Ordinary paprika works if you do not have smoked paprika, but the flavour will be less smoky.
- Keep the pan fairly hot and avoid crowding it so the chicken browns without drying out.
