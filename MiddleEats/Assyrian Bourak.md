# Assyrian Bourak (Crispy Beef Rolls)

**Makes:** 10 small rolls  
**Total:** about 45 minutes

Crisp fried rolls with an Iraqi Assyrian filling of beef mince, onion, parsley and warm spices. This small batch is adapted from [Hilda Sterner’s family recipe](https://hildaskitchenblog.com/recipe/assyrian-egg-rolls-bourak-recipe/).

## Ingredients

- 225 g beef mince (about 10–12% fat)
- ½ small onion, finely chopped
- ½ tsp fine salt
- ½ tsp mild curry powder
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- ½ tsp sweet paprika
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 2 Tbsp finely chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 10 square [egg-roll wrappers](Egg%20Roll%20Wrappers.md), homemade or shop-bought, thawed if frozen
- A little water, for sealing
- Neutral oil, such as sunflower oil, for shallow-frying

## Method

1. Brown the mince in a frying pan over medium-high heat, breaking it up finely with a spoon. Stir in the onion and cook until softened, about 3–4 minutes. Add the salt and spices, stir for 30 seconds, then take the pan off the heat and stir in the parsley. The filling should be fully cooked and fairly dry. Spoon off any pooled fat and spread the filling on a plate to cool.
2. Lay a wrapper in front of you with a corner pointing towards you. Spoon about 1 heaped Tbsp of cooled filling just below the centre. Fold the near corner over the filling, tuck in the sides and roll snugly. Dampen the far corner with water and press it down to seal. Repeat, keeping unused wrappers covered with a clean, slightly damp tea towel.
3. Heat about 5 mm of oil in a frying pan to 185–190°C. Fry in batches, starting seam-side down and turning as needed, until golden and crisp all over, about 3–4 minutes per batch. Let the oil come back up to temperature between batches.
4. Drain on a wire rack or kitchen paper and serve hot, with a chopped tomato and cucumber salad if you like.
