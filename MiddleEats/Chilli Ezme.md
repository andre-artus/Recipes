# Chilli Ezme for Two

**Serves** 2 as a kebab topping

Adapted from [Middle Eats’ döner kebab video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1OB20BOIEQ).

## Ingredients

- ⅙ medium red onion, or about 2 Tbsp roughly chopped
- ½ fresh red chilli
- ⅓ fresh green chilli
- ½ small ripe tomato
- 5 g Turkish red pepper paste (biber salçası)
- A small pinch fine salt
- 1 Tbsp loosely packed flat-leaf parsley leaves, about 1.5 g

## Method

1. Finely chop the tomato and let it drain in a sieve while you prepare the remaining ingredients.
2. Finely chop the onion, chillies and parsley. Remove the chilli seeds and membranes for a milder ezme.
3. Stir everything together with the pepper paste and salt. Spoon the chunky ezme into [lamb döner wraps](Lamb%20Döner%20Kebab.md).
