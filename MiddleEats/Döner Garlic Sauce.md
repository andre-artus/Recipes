# Döner Garlic Sauce for Two

**Serves** 2 as a kebab sauce

Adapted from [Middle Eats’ döner kebab video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1OB20BOIEQ).

## Ingredients

- 30 g mayonnaise
- 30 g plain yoghurt
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- ⅛ tsp dried mint
- ⅛ tsp fine salt
- ½ tsp olive oil
- ⅓ small garlic clove, finely grated

## Method

1. Stir the mayonnaise, yoghurt, lemon juice, mint, salt, olive oil and garlic together until smooth.
2. Chill until ready to spoon over [lamb döner wraps](Lamb%20Döner%20Kebab.md).
