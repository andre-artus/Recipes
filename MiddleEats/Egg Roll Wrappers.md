# Homemade Egg Roll Wrappers

**Makes** 10 squares, about 15 cm across  
**Total** about 1 hour, including a 30-minute rest

Roll these thin wheat-and-egg wrappers by hand and use them raw to wrap the cooled bourak filling. The wrappers cook as the rolls fry. [Egg roll wrapper recipe adapted from YepRecipes](https://www.yeprecipes.com/img-homemade-egg-roll-wrappers-1971.htm)

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain (all-purpose) flour
- ¼ tsp fine table salt
- 1 large egg (about 50 g without the shell), beaten
- 65 g cold water (65 ml), plus a little more if needed
- 1–2 Tbsp cornflour (cornstarch), for rolling and stacking

## Method

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl. Stir in the egg and water, then squeeze the mixture together by hand. If dry crumbs still refuse to come together, add more water 1 tsp at a time. The dough should feel firm.
2. Knead for 5–7 minutes, until the dough is mostly smooth. Cover and rest for 30 minutes at room temperature so it rolls out more easily.
3. Divide into 10 pieces of about 31–32 g. Keep the pieces covered as you work. On a worktop lightly dusted with cornflour, flatten one piece and roll it from the centre outwards into a thin square about 15 cm across and roughly 1 mm thick. Turn it as you roll and use the rolling pin to even out the corners. If the dough springs back, cover it and let it rest for another 5–10 minutes.
4. Lightly dust each wrapper with cornflour before stacking. Keep the stack covered so the edges stay pliable. Brush loose cornflour off the edges before sealing, then fill and fry as directed in the [Assyrian Bourak recipe](Assyrian%20Bourak.md).
