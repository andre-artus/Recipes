# Garlic-Yoghurt Sauce for Two

**Serves:** 2 as a sauce for chicken and rice  
**Preparation:** about 5 minutes  
**Yield:** approximately 80 ml

Adapted from [MiddleEats' chicken marinades video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re-c54S6--o), using the supplied recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1 garlic clove, peeled
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) plain yoghurt
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) mayonnaise
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) dried mint
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) dried oregano
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice

## Method

1. Place the garlic in a small microwave-safe dish and microwave for **15–20 seconds** to mellow it. Watch closely as a single clove can scorch; stop sooner if necessary.
2. Let the garlic cool, then mince or mash it finely.
3. Mix with the yoghurt, mayonnaise, dried mint, dried oregano and lemon juice until combined.
4. Keep chilled until serving with [Joojeh Kebab](Joojeh%20Kebab.md) or [Turkish Marinated Chicken](Turkish%20Marinated%20Chicken.md).

## Adaptation notes

The supplied sauce quantities already make a small bowl suitable for 2 people, so they are unchanged. Mint and oregano are treated as dried herbs in this adaptation.
