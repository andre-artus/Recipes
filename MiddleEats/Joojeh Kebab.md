# Joojeh Kebab for Two

**Serves:** 2, with rice and sauce  
**Preparation:** about 15 minutes  
**Marinating:** 10 minutes minimum; preferably 1 hour, or up to 24 hours in the fridge  
**Cooking:** about 10–16 minutes, depending on the method and chicken thickness

Adapted from [MiddleEats' chicken marinades video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re-c54S6--o), using the supplied recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1 small pinch saffron threads
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) salt, divided
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) boiling water
- 1 Tbsp + ½ tsp (17.5 ml) plain yoghurt
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) ground black pepper
- ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) ground turmeric
- Finely grated zest of ½ lime
- 2½ tsp (12.5 ml) lime juice, measured from the lime
- 1 Tbsp + ½ tsp (17.5 ml) olive oil
- ½ medium onion, thinly sliced
- 300 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into evenly sized pieces about 3 cm across
- A little vegetable oil for the rack, if using the wire-rack method

## Method

1. Grind the saffron with **⅛ tsp of the measured salt** until powdered. Stir in the boiling water and leave to infuse for a few minutes.
2. Mix the yoghurt, remaining **⅜ tsp salt**, black pepper, turmeric, lime zest and measured lime juice in a bowl.
3. Stir in the saffron water and olive oil. Add the onion and chicken, then mix thoroughly to coat.
4. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 10 minutes, preferably 1 hour, or up to 24 hours.
5. Cook using either method below. Discard the used marinade and onion.

### Under the oven grill on skewers

1. Preheat the grill/broiler to high. Use metal skewers and a grill-safe tray or baking dish.
2. Lift the chicken out of the marinade and let the excess drip off. Thread onto the skewers without packing the pieces tightly. Fold thin pieces if necessary so they do not hang far below the skewers.
3. Rest the skewers across the tray or dish so the chicken is suspended above it. Place under the grill, leaving clearance from the element.
4. Grill for about **10–12 minutes** until browned on the first side. Turn and grill for another **2–4 minutes**. Check earlier if your grill is particularly hot.
5. Check that the thickest pieces reach **at least 74°C**; cook longer if needed. If the surface browns too quickly, move the tray farther from the element.

### Under the oven grill on a wire rack

1. Preheat the grill/broiler to high. Place a grill-safe wire rack over an oven tray and lightly brush the rack with vegetable oil.
2. Let excess marinade drip off the chicken, then arrange the pieces on the rack in a single layer with space between them.
3. Grill for about **8–10 minutes** until browned. Turn and grill for another **2–4 minutes**, or until the thickest pieces reach **at least 74°C**. Thicker pieces may need longer; move the tray farther from the element if browning too quickly.

Serve with [Saffron Rice](Saffron%20Rice.md) and [Garlic-Yoghurt Sauce](Garlic-Yoghurt%20Sauce.md).

## Adaptation notes

The original marinade for 500 g chicken is reduced to approximately 60% for **300 g chicken**, or 150 g per person, with practical rounding of small quantities. Use **2½ tsp (12.5 ml) lime juice in total**, plus the zest of ½ lime. The supplied method mentions garlic but gives no quantity in the ingredient list, so it is omitted here. Cooking times depend on piece size, so they are unchanged.

Temperature guidance: [USDA safe internal temperatures](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/What-is-a-safe-internal-temperature-for-cooking-meat-and-poultry).
