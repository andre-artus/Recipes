# Pickled Red Cabbage for Two

**Serves** 2 as a kebab topping  
**Resting** 30 minutes

Adapted from [Middle Eats’ döner kebab video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1OB20BOIEQ).

## Ingredients

- 150 g red cabbage, cored and finely shredded
- ½ tsp + a pinch fine salt
- ¼ tsp food-grade citric acid
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp olive oil

## Method

1. Sprinkle the salt and citric acid over the shredded cabbage. Massage with your hands until the cabbage starts to soften and release liquid.
2. Leave for 30 minutes, then toss with the lemon juice and olive oil. Chill until needed.
3. Lift the cabbage from the bowl with a fork, letting excess dressing drip off before adding it to the [lamb döner wraps](Lamb%20Döner%20Kebab.md).
