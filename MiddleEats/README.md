# MiddleEats Recipes for Two

Adapted from the ingredient lists and directions supplied for [MiddleEats' chicken marinades video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re-c54S6--o).

## Recipes

- [Joojeh Kebab](Joojeh%20Kebab.md) — saffron, lime and yoghurt chicken; serves 2.
- [Turkish Marinated Chicken](Turkish%20Marinated%20Chicken.md) — chicken with onion juice, pepper paste and yoghurt; serves 2.
- [Saffron Rice](Saffron%20Rice.md) — a rice side for 2.
- [Garlic-Yoghurt Sauce](Garlic-Yoghurt%20Sauce.md) — a small bowl of sauce for 2.

## Portions and adaptations

- Choose **one chicken recipe**, then serve with the rice and sauce. Each chicken recipe uses **300 g boneless, skinless chicken**, giving 150 g raw chicken per person. The marinades are reduced to approximately 60% of the supplied quantities, with practical rounding for spoon measures, onion, garlic and saffron.
- To try both marinades in one meal for 2, use **150 g chicken and half of every marinade ingredient for each recipe below**. Make one batch each of rice and sauce.
- The original 2 cups of dry rice have been reduced to **150 g (approximately ¾ cup)** for 2 side portions. Oil and salt are adjusted to practical spoon measures, and the water and cooking method are specified for a small batch.
- The supplied sauce makes enough for 2, so its quantities are unchanged.
- For Joojeh Kebab, use the zest of ½ lime and **2½ tsp (12.5 ml) lime juice in total**. The supplied list names both a lime and lime juice; this adaptation treats the lime as the source of the measured juice. Garlic is omitted because it appears only in the supplied method, with no ingredient quantity.

## Measurements and ingredients

- **1 tsp = 5 ml; 1 Tbsp = 15 ml; ¼ cup oil ≈ 60 ml.** Weigh the rice for consistency.
- Use plain, unsweetened yoghurt and dried mint and oregano for the sauce.
- Turkish red pepper paste is often sold as **biber salçası**. Tomato purée here means concentrated tomato paste, rather than passata.
- Chicken cooking times are guides. Check the thickest pieces with a thermometer: they must reach **at least 74°C**, following [USDA poultry temperature guidance](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/What-is-a-safe-internal-temperature-for-cooking-meat-and-poultry).

## Cooking the meal together

1. Marinate the chicken in the fridge, ideally at least 1 hour before cooking.
2. Make the sauce and keep it chilled.
3. Start the rice about 30 minutes before serving. Preheat the oven grill while it cooks.
4. Grill the chicken while the rice finishes cooking and resting. Serve with the sauce.
