# Saffron Rice for Two

**Serves:** 2 as a side  
**Preparation:** about 5 minutes  
**Cooking and resting:** about 25–30 minutes

Adapted from [MiddleEats' chicken marinades video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re-c54S6--o), using the supplied recipe.

## Ingredients

- 150 g dry basmati rice (approximately ¾ cup)
- 2 tsp (10 ml) neutral oil
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) salt, divided, or to taste
- 250 ml boiling water for cooking the rice, or the amount recommended on your rice packet for 150 g using the absorption method
- 1 small pinch saffron threads
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) boiling water for blooming the saffron

## Method

1. Rinse the rice thoroughly in several changes of cold water until the water is mostly clear. Drain well.
2. Heat the oil in a small, heavy-based saucepan with a tight-fitting lid. Add the drained rice and stir over medium heat for 1–2 minutes.
3. Reserve a few grains of the measured salt for grinding the saffron. Add the rest of the salt and the cooking water to the rice. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover, turn the heat to very low and gently cook for **12–15 minutes**, until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender. Follow the packet cooking time if it differs. If the rice is still firm and the pan is dry, add a small splash of boiling water and cook, covered, a little longer.
5. Take off the heat and leave covered for **10 minutes**, then fluff with a fork.
6. While the rice cooks, grind the saffron with the reserved few grains of salt. Stir in **1 Tbsp (15 ml) boiling water** and leave to infuse.
7. Mix about **½ cup of the cooked rice** with the saffron water until golden. Fold this through the remaining rice or spoon it over the white rice when serving.

Serve with [Joojeh Kebab](Joojeh%20Kebab.md) or [Turkish Marinated Chicken](Turkish%20Marinated%20Chicken.md), plus [Garlic-Yoghurt Sauce](Garlic-Yoghurt%20Sauce.md).

## Adaptation notes

The supplied recipe uses 2 cups of dry rice. This version uses 150 g for 2 side portions, with practical amounts of oil and salt. The measured water, gentle covered cooking and resting step replace the original water-depth and boiling instructions for more predictable results in a small saucepan. Rice brands vary, so use your packet's absorption directions where available.
