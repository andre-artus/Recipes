# Tomato and Parsley Salad for Two

**Serves** 2 as a kebab topping

Adapted from [Middle Eats’ döner kebab video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1OB20BOIEQ).

## Ingredients

- 1 small ripe tomato
- 1 Tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
- A small pinch fine salt
- A small pinch ground sumac

## Method

1. Slice the tomato thinly. Arrange the slices on a plate and scatter with the parsley, salt and sumac.
2. Add to [lamb döner wraps](Lamb%20Döner%20Kebab.md) just before serving.
