# Crispy Fried Shallots

**Serves:** 2 as a curry garnish  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cook:** about 10–15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 50 g peeled shallots
- 75 ml neutral cooking oil, or enough to just cover the shallots in a small saucepan
- A small pinch of fine salt

## Method

1. Slice the shallots evenly, about 1 mm thick. Separate the slices and pat dry thoroughly.
2. Put the shallots and oil in a small saucepan. Heat over medium heat until steadily bubbling, then reduce to medium-low.
3. Fry gently, stirring often, until the slices are pale golden and the bubbling has noticeably slowed, about 10–15 minutes. Watch closely near the end.
4. Lift out promptly with a slotted spoon or metal sieve and spread on kitchen paper. They continue browning after removal; avoid waiting until dark brown.
5. Sprinkle with salt and cool completely to crisp. Divide over the [yellow chicken curry](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md) just before serving.

Make these earlier the same day. Once completely cool, keep airtight until serving. The strained shallot oil can supply the 2 tsp (10 ml) oil for the curry that day.

Reference: [Hot Thai Kitchen — Fried Shallots](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/fried-shallots/).
