# Thai Recipes for Two

The three main recipes serve **2 people**. Together they make a meal of yellow chicken curry, cool cucumber relish and flaky roti. Supporting preparations below specify their yields and how much to use for this meal.

## Recipes

- [Thai Yellow Curry Chicken](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md) — coconut curry with chicken thighs and potatoes; about 45 minutes.
- [Thai Ajad](Thai%20Ajad.md) — sweet-and-sour cucumber relish; about 10 minutes of preparation, plus cooling and resting.
- [Thai Roti](Thai%20Roti.md) — 4 small, flaky flatbreads, 2 per person; about 40 minutes of hands-on preparation and cooking, plus 1 hour 20 minutes of resting.

## Pastes and supporting preparations

- [Thai Yellow Curry Paste](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Paste.md) — one small batch for the curry serving 2; use all of it in place of bought paste.
- [Yellow Curry Spice Mix](Yellow%20Curry%20Spice%20Mix.md) — a small jar of ground spices; use 1 tsp (5 ml) in the paste and save the rest.
- [Tamarind Paste](Tamarind%20Paste.md) — a small batch from tamarind pulp; an optional sour seasoning for the curry. Start with 1 tsp (5 ml).
- [Crispy Fried Shallots](Crispy%20Fried%20Shallots.md) — an optional garnish for 2 portions of curry.

The [ajad recipe](Thai%20Ajad.md) includes its vinegar dressing, and the [roti recipe](Thai%20Roti.md) includes its dough. Fish sauce and fermented shrimp paste are bought fermented seasonings.

## Cooking the meal together

1. Start the roti about **2 hours before serving**. Make the dough, give it its first rest, then divide and oil the balls for the longer rest.
2. During that rest, make the spice mix and curry paste if using homemade. Make the ajad dressing and let it cool. Slice the vegetables and keep them chilled separately. Prepare optional tamarind and fried shallots earlier, or allow extra time before starting the roti.
3. Start the curry about **45 minutes before serving**. Add the cooled dressing to the ajad vegetables about 15 minutes before serving.
4. Once the curry is cooked, cover and keep it hot over very low heat while you stretch and fry the roti. Add a splash of water to the curry if it thickens.
5. Serve the roti straight from the pan, with the curry and chilled ajad. The potatoes and roti provide the starch; rice is optional.

## Measurement and ingredient notes

- **1 tsp = 5 ml; 1 Tbsp = 15 ml.** Spoon measures below include their metric volumes. Weigh flour for the roti.
- Use Thai **yellow curry paste**, rather than yellow sour curry paste or ordinary curry powder. Heat and salt levels vary by brand, so start with the lower quantity suggested.
- Homemade and bought paste have different strengths: use the whole homemade batch (approximately 75–90 g), or 30–40 g bought paste. The homemade paste includes a small amount of spice mix; spice mix alone does not replace curry paste.
- Use unsweetened coconut milk. Plain flour means ordinary cake/all-purpose flour, not self-raising flour.
- Shallots can be replaced with a little thinly sliced red onion.
