# Tamarind Paste

**Makes:** approximately 20–25 ml; enough for one curry for 2, with a little left over  
**Prep:** 5 minutes  
**Soak:** 20 minutes  
**Cook:** 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 10 g sour tamarind pulp from a compressed block
- 4 tsp (20 ml) freshly boiled water
- 1 tsp (5 ml) water for rinsing, plus a little more if needed

## Method

1. Break the pulp into a small heatproof bowl. Add the hot water and soak for 20 minutes, until cool enough to handle.
2. Mash thoroughly. Press through a small sieve into a small saucepan. Rinse the remaining fibres with the extra water, pressing out the pulp. Discard seeds and fibres.
3. Stir over low heat until bubbling, then simmer gently for 4–5 minutes. Add a little water if it becomes too thick to stir.
4. In the [curry for 2](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md), start with **1 tsp (5 ml)**; taste before adding another teaspoon.

Cool and freeze any remainder in teaspoon portions. This is a loose Thai-style cooking paste; very dark, thick bottled concentrates need different quantities.

Reference: [Hot Thai Kitchen — Making Tamarind Paste](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/making-tamarind-paste/).
