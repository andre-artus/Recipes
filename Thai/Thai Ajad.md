# Thai Ajad (Sweet-and-Sour Cucumber Relish)

**Serves:** 2 as a side  
**Prep:** 10 minutes  
**Cooling:** about 15–20 minutes  
**Rest:** 15 minutes

A crisp cucumber relish to serve alongside [Thai Yellow Curry Chicken](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md) and [Thai Roti](Thai%20Roti.md).

## Ingredients

### Dressing

- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) white vinegar or unseasoned rice vinegar
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) water
- 20 g white sugar
- ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) fine salt, or a small pinch

### Vegetables

- 120 g cucumber, quartered lengthways and sliced 3–5 mm thick
- 1 small shallot (about 15 g), thinly sliced, or 15 g red onion
- ½ small red chilli, thinly sliced; use less or omit for a mild relish

## Method

1. Combine the vinegar, water, sugar and salt in a small saucepan. Warm gently, stirring, just until the sugar dissolves. Remove from the heat.
2. Pour into a heatproof bowl and cool completely, about 15–20 minutes. Set the bowl in cold water to cool it faster if needed.
3. Put the cucumber, shallot and chilli in a small serving bowl. Pour over the cooled dressing and toss.
4. Refrigerate for 15 minutes, then serve with some dressing spooned over each portion. Stir before serving; the vegetables do not need to be fully submerged.

## Notes

- Cool the dressing before adding it to the vegetables to preserve their crunch.
- Use unseasoned vinegar: seasoned sushi vinegar already contains sugar and salt.
- If preparing ahead, keep the dressing and vegetables chilled separately and combine 15 minutes before eating.
- Best eaten the day it is assembled.
