# Thai Roti (Flaky Flatbreads)

**Serves:** 2 alongside curry  
**Makes:** 4 small roti, 2 per person  
**Prep and shaping:** about 25 minutes  
**Rest:** 20 minutes, then 1 hour  
**Cook:** 12–16 minutes  
**Total:** about 2 hours

Plain, pan-fried Thai-style roti for scooping up [yellow chicken curry](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md).

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 150 g plain cake/all-purpose flour
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) fine salt
- 1 tsp (5 ml) white sugar
- 10 g softened unsalted butter
- 25 g beaten egg: beat 1 egg, then weigh out 25 g (about half a large egg)
- 60 ml room-temperature water, plus up to 2 tsp (10 ml) more if needed

### Resting, shaping and frying

- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) neutral cooking oil, divided
- 10 g melted unsalted butter

## Method

1. Mix the flour, salt and sugar. Rub in the softened butter. Add the weighed egg and 60 ml water; mix into a dough. Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and pliable. If dry, knead in extra water a teaspoon at a time. It should be soft and slightly tacky, without smearing over your hands.
2. Cover and rest for 20 minutes. Divide into 4 equal pieces and roll into smooth balls. Coat with 1 Tbsp (15 ml) oil, place in a covered dish and rest for 1 hour at a cool room temperature.
3. Lightly oil the worktop with a little of the remaining oil. Flatten one ball, then gently stretch it outwards from the centre into a very thin sheet, about 25 cm across. The edges should be almost translucent. If it springs back, cover and rest for 10 more minutes.
4. Brush with a quarter of the melted butter. Fold the left and right sides towards the centre, overlapping slightly, then fold the top and bottom inwards to form a loose square. Repeat with the remaining balls.
5. Heat a frying pan over medium heat. Use the remaining oil to fry the roti one at a time for about 1½–2 minutes per side, until golden, crisp and cooked through. Lower the heat if they brown too quickly.
6. Serve immediately, 2 per person, with curry and [ajad](Thai%20Ajad.md).

## Notes

- Resting makes the dough easier to stretch. Use oil on the worktop rather than extra flour.
- Keep unused beaten egg covered in the fridge and cook it for another meal.

Technique references: [Temple of Thai — Street Vendor Style Roti](https://www.templeofthai.com/recipes/roti.php) and [Hot Thai Kitchen — Thai Roti](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/banana-roti/). This small-batch version is unfilled and served savoury.
