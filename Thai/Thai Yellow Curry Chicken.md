# Thai Yellow Curry Chicken

**Serves:** 2  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Cook:** 30 minutes

Timing assumes the curry paste is ready. Allow another 25–35 minutes if making it from scratch.

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp (10 ml) neutral cooking oil
- 1 whole batch (approximately 75–90 g) [homemade Thai yellow curry paste](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Paste.md), or 30–40 g bought Thai yellow curry paste
- 300 g skinless, boneless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm pieces
- 400 ml unsweetened coconut milk
- 75 ml water, plus extra if needed
- 200 g potatoes, peeled and cut into 1.5 cm cubes
- 1 small onion (about 80 g), cut into wedges
- 1 tsp (5 ml) fish sauce, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp (5 ml) palm sugar or brown sugar
- 1–2 tsp (5–10 ml) [homemade tamarind paste](Tamarind%20Paste.md), optional, to taste
- 1 batch [crispy fried shallots](Crispy%20Fried%20Shallots.md), optional, to garnish 2 portions

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the curry paste and cook until fragrant: 1–2 minutes for bought paste, or 3–5 minutes for homemade paste, until excess moisture evaporates. Lower the heat if it catches.
2. Add the chicken and stir to coat. Add the coconut milk, water, potatoes, onion, fish sauce and sugar.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer. Partially cover and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender and the chicken reaches **75°C** in the centre. Add water if the sauce becomes too thick.
4. If using tamarind, stir in 1 tsp (5 ml), simmer for 1 minute and taste before adding more. Adjust with a little more fish sauce or sugar. Top with the fried shallots, if using, and serve with [Thai Roti](Thai%20Roti.md) and [Thai Ajad](Thai%20Ajad.md).

## Notes

- This version uses boneless thighs for a shorter cooking time. Cut the potatoes small so they finish with the chicken.
- Ajad supplies a sharp contrast to the rich sauce; serve it alongside in a separate bowl.

Technique reference: [Hot Thai Kitchen — Yellow Curry Chicken](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/yellow-curry-chicken/). Quantities and timing adapted for two with boneless chicken.
