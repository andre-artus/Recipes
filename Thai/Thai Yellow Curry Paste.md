# Thai Yellow Curry Paste

**Makes:** approximately 75–90 g; use the whole batch in [yellow chicken curry for 2](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Chicken.md)  
**Prep:** 25–35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 g mild dried red chillies, stems and seeds removed
- 1 tsp (5 ml) coriander seeds
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) cumin seeds
- ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) white peppercorns
- 10 g trimmed lemongrass, tender inner base only, very finely sliced
- 5 g peeled galangal, finely chopped
- 5 g peeled ginger, finely chopped
- ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) fine salt
- 10 g peeled garlic, chopped
- 25 g peeled shallot, chopped
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) ground turmeric
- 1 tsp (5 ml) [Yellow Curry Spice Mix](Yellow%20Curry%20Spice%20Mix.md), or bought mild curry powder
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) Thai fermented shrimp paste (gapi)
- Up to 2 tsp (10 ml) water, only if needed

## Method

1. Snip the chillies into small pieces. Soak in hot water for 15 minutes, then drain and squeeze dry.
2. Toast the coriander and cumin in a dry pan over medium-low heat for 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Cool, then grind with the peppercorns.
3. In a sturdy mortar, pound the lemongrass, galangal, ginger and salt until fine. Add the chillies, garlic and shallot in stages, pounding between additions.
4. Work in the ground spices, turmeric, spice mix and shrimp paste until smooth. Alternatively, use a small spice-paste grinder, scraping down often and adding water sparingly; a large blender struggles with this quantity.
5. Use the entire batch in the curry. This fresh paste is less concentrated than bought paste; **75–90 g homemade replaces the 30–40 g bought paste in the linked recipe**. Fry it for 3–5 minutes, until fragrant and no longer watery.

## Notes

- Mild dried long red chillies give gentler heat than small dried bird's-eye chillies.
- Frozen galangal and lemongrass work well. If galangal is unavailable, use another 5 g ginger; the flavour will differ.
- Shrimp paste contains shellfish. It is a fermented ingredient to buy ready-made. Omitting it gives a lighter paste; adjust the curry's fish sauce after cooking.
- Refrigerate covered for up to 2 days, or freeze for up to 1 month. Cook before eating.
