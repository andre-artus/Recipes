# Yellow Curry Spice Mix

**Makes:** about 4½ tsp, enough for 4 batches of [yellow curry paste](Thai%20Yellow%20Curry%20Paste.md), each for a curry serving 2  
**Prep:** 5 minutes

A simple house blend to replace the bought curry powder in the paste. Make this small batch and keep the remainder for another meal.

## Ingredients

Use ground spices throughout.

- 2 tsp (10 ml) ground coriander
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (5 ml) ground turmeric
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp (1.25 ml) ground white or black pepper
- ⅛ tsp (0.625 ml) ground cloves

## Method

1. Mix thoroughly, breaking up any lumps.
2. Use **1 tsp (5 ml)** in one batch of the homemade yellow curry paste.
3. Keep the remainder in a small, airtight jar away from heat and light. Label it and use within 3 months for the best aroma.

This blend is one component of the paste; it does not replace its fresh lemongrass, galangal, ginger, garlic or shallots.

Spice reference: [Thai Cookbook TV — Yellow Curry Paste](https://www.thaicookbook.tv/print_recipe.php?rid=13). This is a house blend, rather than a reproduction of that recipe.
