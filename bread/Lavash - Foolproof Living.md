# Lavash (Foolproof Living)

**Makes:** 4 lavash, about 23–25 cm across  
**Rest:** 20 minutes, then another 20 minutes  
**Total:** about 1 hour 10 minutes

Soft, lightly puffed skillet flatbreads with yeast, milk and olive oil. Scaled from 10 to 4 from [Aysegul Sanford’s Foolproof Living recipe](https://foolproofliving.com/lavash-bread-recipe/).

## Ingredients

- 170 g plain (all-purpose) flour, plus a little for dusting and rolling
- ¼ tsp fine table salt (about 1.5 g)
- 71 ml warm water (about 43°C)
- Scant ½ tsp honey (about 2 ml)
- 2 g instant yeast (about ⅝ tsp)
- 1 Tbsp plus 1¾ tsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 49 g whole milk (3 Tbsp plus ½ tsp), at room temperature

## Method

1. Stir the honey into the warm water, then mix in the yeast. Wait about 5 minutes for bubbles to appear. Stir in the olive oil and milk.
2. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl. Add the liquids and knead in the bowl for about 5 minutes. The dough will be sticky. Cover and leave somewhere warm for 20 minutes, until slightly puffed.
3. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured worktop and knead for about 5 minutes, until smooth. Use only enough extra flour to keep it workable.
4. Divide into 4 equal pieces (about 80 g each), shape into balls and dust lightly with flour. Cover and rest for another 20 minutes.
5. Heat a dry, 30 cm cast-iron frying pan over high heat. Roll out one ball at a time to a 23–25 cm round, keeping the rest covered.
6. Lay the round in the hot pan. Flip after about 10 seconds, once bubbles start to form. Keep turning about every 10 seconds until the bread is cooked through and golden in spots, usually after 3–4 flips.
7. Stack the cooked lavash under a clean tea towel as you go to keep them soft. Serve warm.

## Measurement note

Use 15 ml per Tbsp and 5 ml per tsp.
