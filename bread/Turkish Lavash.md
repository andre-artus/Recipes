# Turkish Lavaş (Skillet Flatbread)

**Makes:** 8 flatbreads, about 22–24 cm across  
**Rest:** 30 minutes  
**Total:** about 1 hour

Thin, soft flatbreads for rolling into dürüm with grilled meat, sliced onions and herbs.

## Ingredients

- 300 g plain flour (cake or all-purpose flour), plus extra for rolling
- 5 g fine salt (about ¾ tsp)
- 175 ml lukewarm water, plus a little more if needed
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) olive oil

## Method

1. Mix the flour and salt. Add the water and olive oil and mix until a dough forms. Knead for 5–7 minutes until smooth. If dry flour remains or the dough cracks as you knead, add water 1 tsp at a time.
2. Divide into 8 pieces of about 62 g each and roll into balls. Cover with a clean tea towel and rest for 30 minutes.
3. Heat a dry heavy frying pan over medium-high heat. On a lightly floured worktop, roll one ball into a thin round, about 22–24 cm across or small enough to lie flat in your pan. Keep the other balls covered.
4. Shake off excess flour and lay the round in the hot pan. Cook for 30–45 seconds, until bubbles rise and light brown spots appear underneath. Flip and cook for another 15–30 seconds, until the second side is lightly spotted. If the bread darkens before it bubbles, lower the heat a little.
5. Transfer straight to a plate lined with a clean tea towel and fold the towel over it. Roll and cook the remaining rounds, stacking them under the towel as you go. Let the covered stack sit for 5 minutes so the steam softens the breads before serving.

## Notes

- If the dough keeps springing back as you roll, cover it and give it another 5–10 minutes to rest.
- Rewarm cooled lavaş briefly in a dry pan. If it feels stiff, mist it lightly with water first.
