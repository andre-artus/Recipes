# Fresh Egg Pasta

## Ingredients

* Whole eggs, weighed without their shells
* 2 g tipo 00 flour or plain flour per 1 g of egg, plus a little extra for dusting

**Flour (g) = egg (g) × 2**

| Egg | Flour |
| ---: | ---: |
| 50 g | 100 g |
| 110 g | 220 g |
| 150 g | 300 g |

## Method

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and weigh them. Beat with a fork, then mix in the calculated amount of flour until a shaggy dough forms.
2. Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and firm. If the dough still crumbles after a few minutes of kneading, work in water a few drops at a time. If it stays sticky, dust it lightly with flour.
3. Wrap the dough and let it rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes.
4. Divide the dough into portions and keep the unused portions covered. Roll each portion into a thin sheet with a rolling pin or pasta machine, then cut into noodles. Dust lightly with flour to keep them from sticking.
5. Cook in boiling salted water. Start tasting after 1 minute and drain when tender, usually after 1–3 minutes depending on thickness.
